## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**Does this look AI-generated? No — and further from it than in July.** Committed olive/terracotta/parchment
with a written contrast contract. Zilla Slab + Work Sans, neither reflex-reject. No gradient text, no
glassmorphism, no hero-metric template, no tracked-uppercase eyebrow per section, no side-stripe borders, no
numbered section scaffolding. Copy refuses to invent proof.

Two residual drifts:
- **Three pages carry zero imagery** — `/services`, `/pricing`, `/faq` are pure text. The brand register is
  explicit that text-only pages where typography carries all visual weight are the failure mode.
- **The scroll-progress bar is dashboard chrome**, which PRODUCT.md rejects by name. It is also the first
  terracotta a visitor meets, before any CTA.

**Deterministic scan** (`detect.mjs --json src`, exit 0, 10 findings): all 10 are the same known false
positives the 2026-07-28 run recorded. Worth writing to `ignore.md`.

July's four P1s are genuinely closed, and the closing bands were the right fix. The score moved 30 -> 31, which

## Priority Issues

### [P1] The consent banner lands on the decisive element of four pages

Bottom-fixed with no space reserved, and it persists until answered.

| Page | What it covers | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `/about` | **The founder's face** | Portrait y=418-848; banner y=505-590 |
| `/pricing` | **The R1,200 build fee** | `elementsFromPoint` returns `pricing-buildfee`, `pricing-buildfee-body` |
| `/` | Founder trust strip (name + photo) | Banner overlays the `hero-founder` block |
| `/services` | First service's body copy, mid-sentence | "jargon, no homework" occluded |

On `/about` the page's argument is "A real person, not a call centre" and the notice covers the person.

**Why it matters:** first thirty seconds of every first visit. The component is deliberately non-blocking so a
visitor can ignore it — but ignoring is the one choice that never resolves, so occlusion is permanent for exactly
the visitor the design was protecting.

**Fix:** reserve space instead of overlaying — `padding-block-end` on `main` while consent is unanswered. Or defer
the notice to first CTA interaction; `analytics.js:100` sends nothing without consent, so nothing is lost.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

### [P1] The One Action Rule has collapsed

DESIGN.md: "If a visitor cannot find the one terracotta thing within two seconds, the page has no primary action."
One homepage scroll contains ~24 terracotta marks, four of which are CTAs:
ScrollProgress.css:11 (1, always visible), Header.css:133 (1), Hero.css:26 (1), base.css:205 (3 CTAs),
CaseStudy.css:32 (1), HowItWorks.css:34 (per step), Values.jsx:40,56,73 (3), Pricing.css:50,69 (2),
**Pricing.css:111 (9 table ticks)**, StickyWhatsApp.css:40 (1).

**DESIGN.md contradicts itself:** the Named Rule reserves terracotta for "the primary CTA and the wordmark";
its colour spec three paragraphs earlier lists "Primary CTAs, the wordmark's 'bos', step numerals, form error
states, the script kicker." The code followed the looser reading and went past it.

**Why it matters:** rendered, Bloom's terracotta R699/mo and its terracotta border compete with the CTA for the
same glance. The site's single success metric is a WhatsApp click.

**Fix:** decide which reading is real; make DESIGN.md state only that. Cheap 50%: nine table ticks and three
Values icon strokes -> `--color-primary` (olive already carries ticks at PricingTiers.css:95); delete the scroll bar.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable colorize`

### [P1] Same three tiers, two visual languages, one click apart

Unresolved since July. `/` renders bordered, shadowed, hover-lifting cards (Pricing.css:32-52); `/pricing` renders
open columns with a sage wash (PricingTiers.css). tokens.css and DESIGN.md ratified the *shadow* as a documented
mockup exception; the *divergence* was never decided. DESIGN.md calls the open row the signature component and
the homepage is the one page that doesn't use it, for the content that matters most.

**Why it matters:** a visitor moving homepage -> pricing re-parses the same offer in a new costume. Rendered, the
card version reads as "software" — the association PRODUCT.md's anti-references reject.

**Fix:** pick one. Continued drift is the worst of the three options.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish`

### [P1] `/services`, `/pricing` and `/faq` ship zero imagery

The bands fixed width, not the nothing-to-look-at problem. Rendered `/services` shows a second symptom: the
hairlines run the full 1152px container while content is capped at ~64ch (~715px). The price lands mid-row with
440px of empty rule to its right, reading as an unfinished table rather than a ledger entry.

**Why it matters:** `/pricing` is the page the SEO work targets. A visitor arriving cold gets prices and prose and
no evidence the work exists.

**Fix:** the delivered Bossie's Gym screenshot belongs on `/services`. Either bound hairlines to the content
measure, or push the price to the rule's right edge so rule and row agree.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

## Minor Observations

- **`/analytics` contradicts its own docstring.** AnalyticsPage.css:1-5 says it "gets no hero, no CTA and no
  persuasion" — but the global Header renders the full marketing nav AND the terracotta CTA on it, and the consent
  banner appears too, asking the owner to consent to tracking on their own admin page.
- base.css:50 sets `font-weight: 700` on h1-h4 while tokens.css:78 states headings "go back to 600". Third session
  outstanding; both files carry comments admitting it.
- The disabled "Show me" button (opacity 0.55 on olive) reads as a pale normal button, not a disabled one.
- `/analytics` shows no loading state beyond the button label; previous results stay on screen during a new query.
- The homepage contact section leaves a large void under the WhatsApp CTA beside a three-field form.
- The SEO panel's olive leaf renders as a soft blob rather than reading as a leaf.
- Only two `:active` states exist site-wide. `.btn-accent` (hero, contact, 404, page-foot CTAs) has no touch press
  feedback; `.sticky-whatsapp-btn` does.

## Questions to Consider

- The banner is the most carefully reasoned component here and is doing the most visible damage. What if consent
  were asked at the WhatsApp tap, where the visitor is already thinking about contact?
- DESIGN.md states the One Action Rule then immediately lists five uses for terracotta. Which did the brand mean?
- The homepage broke the no-card rule on the mockup's authority. Is the rule "no cards", or "no cards except where
  a mockup said so" — and which of those is a system?
- If `/pricing` is the page the SEO work targets, why is it the page with the least evidence on it?
